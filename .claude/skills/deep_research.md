# Deep Research Skill

## Purpose
When the agent needs to look up information—for any research task, question, or topic—it MUST conduct exhaustive, multi-perspective research before drawing conclusions or producing work. Superficial or single-source lookups are forbidden.

## Required Protocol

### 1. Query Expansion & Reframing
Before conducting any searches, expand the original query into multiple variations:
- **Rephrase**: Use synonyms, different terminology, and alternative phrasings (e.g., "climate change" → "global warming", "anthropogenic climate effects")
- **Reframe**: Approach from different angles (e.g., economic impact vs. scientific consensus vs. policy debate vs. historical context)
- **Narrow/Broaden**: Try both specific sub-topics and broader categories
- **Perspective shift**: Explicitly seek contrarian, critical, international, or minority viewpoints
- **Temporal**: Include "latest", "2024/2025", "history of", "evolution of" as relevant

### 2. Search Execution
Execute searches for EACH variation. Do not stop after one or two queries. Aim for:
- **Minimum 5–7 distinct search queries** covering different phrasings and angles
- **Diverse source types**: academic, news, industry, government, NGO, international
- **Cross-validate**: When multiple sources agree, note consensus; when they disagree, surface the disagreement

### 3. Information Collection Criteria
Continue searching until you have:
- At least 3–5 unique, high-quality sources per major subtopic
- Explicit representation of differing or conflicting perspectives
- Coverage of: definitions, key facts, debates, recent developments, notable experts/institutions
- Identification of gaps, uncertainties, or areas where information is thin

### 4. Synthesis Before Action
Before writing, answering, or coding:
- Compile all findings into a mental or written outline
- Note which sources support which claims
- Identify the strongest evidence and weakest claims
- Only then proceed to document, summarize, or build

## When This Skill Applies
- **Always** when the user asks about a topic the agent does not have current, comprehensive knowledge of
- **Always** when starting a new research topic for the knowledge base
- **Always** when expanding an existing topic with new subtopics

## Antitheses (What NOT to Do)
- Do NOT perform a single search and assume it is sufficient
- Do NOT rely on one perspective or one type of source
- Do NOT skip reframing or rephrasing the query
- Do NOT proceed to documentation before collecting exhaustive information
